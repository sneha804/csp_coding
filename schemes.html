PM-YASASVI Scholarship Scheme

Education | Ministry of Social Justice & Empowerment

The scheme provides financial assistance to meritorious students from Other Backward Classes (OBC), Economically Backward Classes (EBC) and DNT communities for higher education.

95% Match

About the Scheme

PM-YASASVI Scholarship Scheme (PM Young Achievers Scholarship Award Scheme for a Vibrant India for OBCs and Others) is a Government of India initiative under the Ministry of Social Justice and Empowerment that provides educational and financial support to students belonging to OBC, EBC, and DNT communities. The scheme aims to help eligible students continue their education by providing assistance for school and higher education, including scholarships and support for educational expenses. It covers components such as Pre-Matric Scholarship, Post-Matric Scholarship, Top Class School Education, Top Class College Education, and hostel facilities. The scheme helps reduce financial barriers and encourages students from disadvantaged communities to pursue their education.

Benefits

  • Financial assistance up to ₹75,000 per year
  • Tuition fee and other academic expenses
  • Support for professional and technical courses

Eligibility

  • Age: 18 – 30 years
  • Family income: Below ₹6 Lakh per annum
  • Resident of India
  • Belongs to OBC / EBC / DNT category
  • Enrolled in recognized institution

Required Documents

  • Aadhaar Card
  • Income Certificate
  • Caste Certificate
  • Bonafide Certificate from Institution
  • Bank Account Details
