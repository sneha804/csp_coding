PM-YASASVI Scholarship Scheme

A government scholarship scheme designed to support students from socially and economically disadvantaged communities in pursuing quality education.

About the Scheme

The PM-YASASVI Scholarship Scheme is a Government of India initiative aimed at providing financial assistance to students belonging to eligible disadvantaged communities. The scheme supports students in continuing their education and helps reduce financial barriers to quality schooling.

Benefits

Financial assistance for eligible students.
Supports continuation of school education.
Helps students meet educational expenses.
Encourages students to pursue higher education.

Eligibility

  • Students must belong to the eligible beneficiary categories specified under the scheme.
  • Applicants must satisfy the prescribed educational requirements.
  • Family income must fall within the applicable income limit.
  • Other conditions specified by the implementing authority must be fulfilled.

Required Documents

Aadhaar Card
Caste Certificate
Income Certificate
School / College Certificate
Bank Account Details
Passport Size Photograph
